# Evidence of behavior consistent with self-interest and altruism in an artificially intelligent agent

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.02330
First opened: 2026-09-15

AI-generated from selected main-body sections; verify against the full paper.

## Summary points

- In comprehensible non-social trials, text-davinci-003 maximized its token payoff 92% of the time, versus 16–19% for the three less-capable agents.
- In the dictator game, text-davinci-003 shared a median 29.8% of its endowment, while the other agents shared median proportions of only 0.3–1.0%.
- Text-davinci-003 shared substantially more with other AI agents than with the human experimenter or charity; this recipient effect was statistically significant only for that model.

## Main takeaway

Among the four tested models, only text-davinci-003 produced reliable behavior consistent with both self-interest and recipient-sensitive altruism, without establishing genuine altruistic intent.
