# Training Large Language Models to Reason in a Continuous Latent Space

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06769
First opened: 2026-09-15

AI-generated from selected main-body sections; verify against the full paper.

## Summary points

- Coconut replaces tokenized intermediate reasoning with recurrent continuous thoughts by feeding each last hidden state back as the next input embedding, while switching between latent and language modes.
- Experiments report that six continuous thoughts raise GSM8k accuracy from 16.5% without chain-of-thought to 34.1%; Coconut also uses fewer generated tokens while outperforming language-based chain-of-thought on ProntoQA and ProsQA, though not on GSM8k.
- Effective latent reasoning depended on a multi-stage curriculum guided by language reasoning chains; direct question-and-answer training without that curriculum performed no better than the no-chain-of-thought baseline.

## Main takeaway

Continuous latent reasoning can improve reasoning accuracy and token efficiency, but the reported method still relies on carefully staged supervision from language-based reasoning traces.
