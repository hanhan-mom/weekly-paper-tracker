# Looking beyond the next token

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.11336
First opened: 2026-09-20

AI-generated from selected main-body sections; verify against the full paper.

## Summary points

- Trelawney augments training sequences with delimited future tokens or subgoals while retaining the standard decoder architecture, teacher-forcing objective, and a mixture of regular and augmented text.
- On synthetic path-planning tasks, the augmentation improved ordinary autoregressive generation on shorter graphs; on longer graphs, randomly selected model-generated subgoals helped planning, while user-provided subgoals improved controllability.
- In conditional story generation, Trelawney reached specified future goals far more often than few-shot baselines, while WikiText perplexity remained close to standard next-token training.

## Main takeaway

Rearranging training data to expose future goals can improve planning and controllable generation without architectural changes or a noticeable loss in general language-modeling ability.
