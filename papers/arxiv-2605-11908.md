# Delightful Gradients Accelerate Corner Escape

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11908
First opened: 2026-09-20

AI-generated from selected main-body sections; verify against the full paper.

## Summary points

- Delightful Policy Gradient gates each update by advantage times action surprisal; at zero temperature it removes negative-advantage actions, preventing them from reinforcing suboptimal softmax corners.
- For tabular bandits, the paper proves logarithmic corner-escape time for the zero-temperature method, versus potentially exponential transients for standard policy gradient, and reports near-linear escape-time scaling for finite-temperature DG.
- The tabular guarantees do not automatically extend to shared function approximation: a two-state counterexample yields a suboptimal interior fixed point, although MNIST contextual-bandit experiments still show faster recovery than standard policy gradient.

## Main takeaway

Delight-based gating can substantially accelerate escape from suboptimal corners in tabular softmax policies, but shared parameters can invalidate the theoretical mechanism.
