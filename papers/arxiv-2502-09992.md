# Large Language Diffusion Models

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09992
First opened: 2026-09-16

AI-generated from selected main-body sections; verify against the full paper.

## Summary points

- LLaDA replaces left-to-right prediction with masked diffusion: it randomly masks tokens during training and iteratively predicts masked tokens in parallel using bidirectional context.
- Scaling experiments on six tasks found LLaDA broadly competitive with autoregressive baselines trained on the same data, with stronger results on some benchmarks such as MMLU and GSM8K but weaker results on others such as PIQA.
- The 8B base model surpassed LLaMA2 7B on nearly all reported benchmarks and was broadly competitive with LLaMA3 8B; supervised fine-tuning improved most measured tasks, while reversal-completion cases indicated an advantage from non-autoregressive conditioning.

## Main takeaway

The reported experiments show that an 8B masked-diffusion language model can scale and acquire in-context, instruction-following, and reversal capabilities without autoregressive next-token generation.
