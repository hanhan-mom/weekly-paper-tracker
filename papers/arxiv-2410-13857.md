# How Numerical Precision Affects Arithmetical Reasoning Capabilities of LLMs

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13857
First opened: 2026-09-19

AI-generated from selected main-body sections; verify against the full paper.

## Summary points

- For bounded-depth Transformers, the paper proves that constant low precision requires super-polynomial model size for iterated addition and integer multiplication, while standard precision permits constant-size models for addition tasks and quadratic-size models for multiplication.
- Experiments with trained Transformers found float32 consistently more accurate than bfloat16 as arithmetic inputs grew, with the gap especially pronounced for iterated addition and multiplication.
- On LLaMA-3.1-8B Instruct, int4 quantization reduced exact-answer accuracy relative to bfloat16 across tested arithmetic tasks; QLoRA fine-tuning did not always recover the standard-precision model's performance.

## Main takeaway

Lower numerical precision substantially limits reliable arithmetic computation in Transformers, particularly for multi-number addition and multiplication as problem size increases.
