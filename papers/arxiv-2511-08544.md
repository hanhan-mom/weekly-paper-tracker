# LeJEPA: Provable and Scalable Self-Supervised Learning Without the Heuristics

## Summary points

- LeJEPA combines cross-view embedding prediction with regularization toward an isotropic Gaussian distribution to prevent complete and dimensional collapse without teacher–student networks, stop-gradients, or asymmetric architectures.
- SIGReg matches embeddings to the Gaussian target through one-dimensional projections and goodness-of-fit tests, providing a tractable architecture-agnostic regularizer.
- The paper reports competitive or better results across diverse architectures and scales, including nearly billion-parameter models, and says direct in-domain pretraining on Galaxy10 and Food101 outperformed DINOv2-based transfer learning.

## Main takeaway

The paper presents isotropic Gaussian regularization as a theoretically motivated way to simplify JEPA training while retaining competitive large-scale and small-domain performance.
