# Flow Map Language Models: One-step Language Modeling via Continuous Denoising

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16813
First opened: 2026-09-22

AI-generated from selected main-body sections; verify against the full paper.

## Summary points

- The method represents tokens as one-hot vectors and learns a deterministic continuous flow, avoiding the factorized reverse transitions that cause discrete diffusion models to lose token correlations with few sampling steps.
- On LM1B and OpenWebText, the many-step FLM achieved lower generative perplexity than the tested discrete, continuous, and hybrid baselines; its distilled FMLM also substantially outperformed distilled discrete baselines at one to four steps while keeping entropy closer to the datasets.
- The approach supports continuous inference-time guidance, but its full-vocabulary one-hot computation increased training time and memory by about 30% relative to embedding-diffusion methods.

## Main takeaway

The experiments support continuous flow-map language modeling as a strong parallel-generation approach, particularly at one-step and other very low step counts, albeit with higher training cost.
