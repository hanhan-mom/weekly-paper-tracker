# Score Centering Stabilizes Off-policy Reinforcement Learning

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20807
First opened: 2026-09-20

AI-generated from selected main-body sections; verify against the full paper.

## Summary points

- Training–inference mismatch arises when rollout and training policies differ because of numerical precision, engine implementation, or stale asynchronous rollouts.
- Importance sampling can correct this mismatch but increases gradient variance; practical clipping or masking reduces instability while introducing bias, whereas score centering uses an additive correction without importance ratios.
- Experiments report that score centering becomes comparatively stronger as sampler quantization worsens, and top-k approximations match full-vocabulary centering, though several costly 30B comparisons used only one seed.

## Main takeaway

The excerpts support score centering as a practical additive method for stabilizing off-policy RL under training–inference mismatch, with promising but partly limited experimental evidence.
