# ELF: Embedded Language Flows

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10938
First opened: 2026-09-22

## Summary points

- ELF performs continuous-time Flow Matching in contextual token-embedding space, keeping denoising continuous and converting embeddings to tokens only at the final step with the same network rather than a separate decoder.
- Ablations favor pretrained contextual embeddings and the shared denoiser-decoder design; stochastic SDE-like sampling lowers generative perplexity especially with few steps, while larger ELF models improve the quality-diversity frontier.
- On the reported generation, translation, and summarization evaluations, ELF achieves a strong quality-efficiency trade-off versus tested continuous and discrete diffusion language models, using fewer sampling steps and training tokens without distillation.

## Main takeaway

The reported evidence suggests that a minimalist continuous embedding-space flow model with final-step-only discretization can be competitive with leading discrete diffusion language models while improving sampling and training efficiency.
