# Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442
First opened: 2026-09-20

AI-generated from selected main-body sections; verify against the full paper.

## Summary points

- The proposed architecture combines a long-term memory stream, relevance/recency/importance-based retrieval, reflection that synthesizes higher-level inferences, and hierarchical planning to support coherent agent behavior over time.
- In controlled evaluation, the complete architecture received the highest believability score; removing reflection, planning, and memory progressively reduced performance, with nearly all pairwise differences statistically significant.
- In a two-day simulation of 25 agents, the system exhibited information diffusion, relationship formation, and coordination, but agents sometimes missed relevant memories, retrieved incomplete fragments, or embellished remembered facts.

## Main takeaway

Memory retrieval, reflection, and planning together produced more believable individual and emergent social behavior than ablated systems, though retrieval failures and hallucinated embellishments remained important limitations.
